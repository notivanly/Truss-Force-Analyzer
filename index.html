THE STIFFNESSMETHOD

Every member is a spring that only carries axial force. Assemble the springs, apply the loads, solve for the joint movements, and the forces follow.

Member stiffnessk = EA / L
[c s −c −s]ᵀ [c s −c −s] · k

c, s = direction cosines of the member

Solve for joint movementsK u = F

K = assembled stiffness, F = joint loads, u = displacements at the free joint directions

Member forceN = (EA / L) · [−c −s c s] · u

Positive = tension, negative = compression

Reactions and checksR = K u − F
m + r − 2j = degree

m members, r reaction components, j joints. Zero means statically determinate.

Model: linear elastic, 2D pin-jointed truss, small displacement. Loads act at joints. Supports are a pin (x/y constrained) or vertical roller (y constrained). Not intended for real-world structural design or safety decisions.
